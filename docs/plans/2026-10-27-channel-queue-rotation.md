# Queue-Based Account Rotation Across All Account Channels

Scope confirmed by the user: all EAC router account channels; only explicit queueing responses are recognised; switching happens between enabled accounts of the same channel and the same model; existing authentication and quota handling are preserved; nothing is replayed after output; local fixtures are used and no real channel is called.

Covering LobsterAI, Qoder, Qoder China, TRAE, Cline, Loomy, Raccoon, MiniMax, Gemini and ZCode, plus the OpenCode account integration when enabled.

Decision: reuse #663's Qoder-specific handling; the remaining adapters share request-level retry and account selection. Wire into the adapters' registration entry points, covering both direct streams and streams returned by prepareCall. Use AsyncLocalStorage to isolate each request's credentials, tried accounts and retry budget, without changing the shared default account.

An explicit queueing signal is a structured isQueued=true or a queued/model_queued error type. An HTTP status code, Retry-After or ordinary busy text does not by itself mean queueing. Each protocol parser throws the same kind of queueing error once it recognises one; the original authentication, quota, region and other errors continue through their original logic.

By default, follow Qoder: try rotating accounts after three queueing responses or about 20 seconds, with a limited total count and waiting budget. When the account pool is empty, continue with cancellable waiting; queueing does not write a rate-limit marker. Reuse the existing account refresh entry point, refreshing the account the current request actually uses.

No new UI, user configuration or dependencies are added, and no other channel's own authentication, endpoint fallback or quota-based rotation strategy is changed. Unknown upstream queueing messages are not guessed at.
